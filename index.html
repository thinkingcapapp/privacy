Thinking Cap Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

In short: Thinking Cap has no accounts and doesn't track you. Your puzzles, times and streaks stay on your device. We don't collect, sell or share your personal information. The only outside services involved are Apple (purchases and Game Center) and, for free players, Google's ad service, which shows non-personalized ads only.

Who we are

Thinking Cap is a daily logic-puzzle app for iPhone and iPad, made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at thinkingcap@tiptonshome.com.

Information stored on your device

To run the app, Thinking Cap keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your puzzle progress, solve times, hints used and streaks.
  • Your settings, such as favorite games, filters, haptics and mistake highlighting.
  • Whether you have Thinking Cap Pro, so it works offline.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Thinking Cap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple whether you own Pro. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Thinking Cap submits your daily puzzle times and Daily Score to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and unlocks achievements, so you can compare with friends. Your Game Center nickname and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app works fully without Game Center, and you can sign out in the Settings app.

Advertising

If you play for free, Thinking Cap shows a limited number of ads from Google AdMob, never during a puzzle. Pro removes ads.

  • We request non-personalized ads only. Ads are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • Thinking Cap never asks to track you and does not access your device's advertising identifier.
  • To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as your IP address, device type and operating system version. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics, and we don't receive your data.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Thinking Cap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the way Thinking Cap handles information changes, for example when optional iCloud sync of your progress is added, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email thinkingcap@tiptonshome.com.
